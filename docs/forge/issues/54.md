# HMM-20 — VariationalCategoricalHmm - categorical emissions learned by Variational Bayes

> Forgejo issue #54 · closed · opened 2026-10-08 05:15:58 UTC by `claude` · closed 2026-10-08 06:16:49 UTC  
> Labels: `state/merged` · Milestone: J4

**Spec (authoritative):** [`.factory/tickets/HMM-20.yml`](https://github.com/Simon-Bu/hmmlearn-net/blob/main/.factory/tickets/HMM-20.yml)

**Objective.** Port VariationalCategoricalHMM of hmmlearn/vhmm.py to C#: the class HmmLearn.VariationalCategoricalHmm (derived from VariationalBaseHmm)
with the Dirichlet prior and posterior of each emission row, their random initialization and validation, the subnormalized emission
log likelihood of the E-step, the M-step and the lower bound. It reuses the categorical helpers of CategoricalEmission.
Reference: reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/vhmm.py lines 19-231 (VariationalCategoricalHMM) and reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/_emissions.py
lines 30-110 (BaseCategoricalHMM); its tests reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/tests/test_variational_categorical.py; and the numbers of
oracle/golden/variational_categorical.json (structure in oracle/README.md; do not open the JSON files whole, they are large).

**Criteria**

- **C1** — Fit with explicit initial parameters reproduces the six prior and posterior arrays, startprob, transmat, emissionprob, history,
n_iter_done, converged and score of every fit case of oracle/golden/variational_categorical.json with both implementations
(all of s, t and e, e with t, s with t, a few iterations, sequences of length 1).
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm20.FitParityTests`

- **C2** — Init fills the emission prior from the scalar prior or 1 / NFeatures and draws an emission posterior with rows summing to
(sum of lengths) / NComponents, reproducibly for a given RandomState and only when 'e' must be initialized; Check rejects
emission arrays that are null or of different shapes or not (NComponents x NFeatures) with ArgumentException; NFeatures is
inferred from the largest symbol or respected when given; symbols that are fractional, negative or above NFeatures - 1 are
rejected; the scalar priors 1, 2 and 3 given to the constructor end up in the three prior arrays.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm20.InitAndCheckTests`

- **C3** — EstepBegin computes the emission log subnormalization with digamma, the subnormalized log likelihood of a sample is the entry of
the symbol, DoMStep with 'e' sets the posterior to prior + Obs and EmissionProb to its row-normalization (and leaves them alone
without 'e'), ComputeLowerBound adds the emission KL terms to the base bound, and Score, Decode and Sample use EmissionProb after Fit.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm20.EmissionTests`

- **C4** — The lower bound never decreases during Fit (single-iteration fits on data sampled from a three-state cyclic model, both
implementations, random initialization with a fixed seed), sequences of length 1 are accepted, and both implementations agree.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm20.BehaviourTests`

- **C5** — The code stays readable: short methods, no dead code, the XML summaries match the behaviour. · judged by the reviewer
