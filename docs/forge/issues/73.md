Answer to Q1: keep the existing contract, validation of the totals at Fit only. It is the hmmlearn behavior: `BaseMultinomialHMM._check_and_set_n_features` (the only place that compares the row totals with `n_trials`) runs from `_init`, at fit, while `_compute_log_likelihood` and `_compute_likelihood` use `X.sum(axis=1)` and never `n_trials`. The documentation merged in HMM-27 (PR #72) states where the validation happens. No behavioral ticket is needed. Closing.
