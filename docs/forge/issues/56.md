# HMM-21 — VariationalGaussianHmm - parameters, initialization, E-step term, lower bound, scoring and sampling

> Forgejo issue #56 · closed · opened 2026-10-08 06:18:25 UTC by `claude` · closed 2026-10-08 07:58:05 UTC  
> Labels: `state/merged` · Milestone: J4

**Spec (authoritative):** [`.factory/tickets/HMM-21.yml`](https://github.com/Simon-Bu/hmmlearn-net/blob/main/.factory/tickets/HMM-21.yml)

**Objective.** Port VariationalGaussianHMM of hmmlearn/vhmm.py to C#, everything except the M-step of the means and covariances (next ticket): the class
HmmLearn.VariationalGaussianHmm (derived from VariationalBaseHmm) with the Normal-Wishart priors and posteriors of the four covariance
types, their initialization from k-means, their validation, the subnormalized emission log likelihood of the E-step, the variational lower
bound, scoring, decoding and sampling. With params that contain only "s" and "t" the model already fits (the means and covariances stay
at their initial posteriors); "m" and "c" are updated by the next ticket.
Reference: reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/vhmm.py lines 233-695 and 786-844 (VariationalGaussianHMM: __init__, covars_, means_,
_init, _get_n_fit_scalars_per_param, _check, _compute_subnorm_log_likelihood, _compute_lower_bound, the two _needs_ methods) and
reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/_emissions.py lines 112-182 (BaseGaussianHMM); its tests
reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/tests/test_variational_gaussian.py; and the numbers of oracle/golden/variational_gaussian_<type>.json
(spherical, diag, tied and full; structure in oracle/README.md; do not open the JSON files whole, they are large).

**Criteria**

- **C1** — Fit of the "start-and-transitions-only" case of each of the four oracle files (spherical, diag, tied, full) reproduces startprob_posterior,
transmat_posterior, startprob, transmat, history (the lower bound of every iteration, which includes the emission terms), n_iter_done,
converged and score with both implementations, and leaves the means, beta, dof, scale and covars arrays equal to their initial values.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm21.StartAndTransitionsParityTests`

- **C2** — Init, for each covariance type, on sampled two-feature data: MeansPosterior are k-means centers inside the data range, BetaPosterior
and DofPosterior (non-tied) are the cluster sizes (summing to the number of samples), the tied dof is the number of samples, the priors take
their defaults (means prior = data mean, beta 1, dof nf, scale 1e-3 in the shape of the type) or the constructor values, Covars and
ScalePosterior have the compact shape of the type with ScalePosterior = covariance times dof; the same RandomState reproduces the result and
InitParams without 'm' or 'c' leaves the explicit arrays alone. Check rejects (ArgumentException) arrays that are null or have the wrong
shape or length, and an undefined covariance type; NFeatures is set by Fit.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm21.InitAndCheckTests`

- **C3** — ComputeSubnormLogLikelihood has shape (n_samples, n_components), is finite, is the same for the spherical, diag, full and tied
parameterizations of one and the same isotropic model, equals the closed form of vhmm.py for a one-feature model computed in the test with
MathNet.Numerics DiGamma, and gets larger when a sample moves toward a posterior mean; ComputeLowerBound equals the base lower bound minus
non-negative KL terms for the emissions and the bound of a model whose posteriors equal its priors adds no emission term.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm21.EmissionTermTests`

- **C4** — GetNFitScalarsPerParam gives the documented counts for each type, Score, Decode, Predict and PredictProba work on a model whose
Means and Covars are set explicitly (Score equals the sum of the log likelihoods given by Stats.LogMultivariateNormalDensity combined with
the forward pass for a one-state model), Sample returns states and observations of the right shapes whose empirical mean and covariance
are close to Means and Covars for a one-state model (fixed seed, wide tolerance), the Covars setter validates and copies, and FullCovars
has the full shape for every type.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm21.ModelTests`

- **C5** — The code stays readable: short methods, no dead code, the XML summaries match the behaviour. · judged by the reviewer
