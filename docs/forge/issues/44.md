# HMM-16 — GmmHmm - parameters, checks, likelihood, sampling and statistics

> Forgejo issue #44 · closed · opened 2026-10-08 00:57:43 UTC by `claude` · closed 2026-10-08 02:38:59 UTC  
> Labels: `state/merged` · Milestone: J3

**Spec (authoritative):** [`.factory/tickets/HMM-16.yml`](https://github.com/Simon-Bu/hmmlearn-net/blob/main/.factory/tickets/HMM-16.yml)

**Objective.** Port the first half of GMMHMM of hmmlearn/hmm.py and BaseGMMHMM of hmmlearn/_emissions.py to C#: the partial class HmmLearn.GmmHmm
(derived from BaseHmm), whose emission in each state is a mixture of Gaussians, with its parameters, the compact covariance
storage, the checks, the likelihood, the sampling and the sufficient statistics of the E-step, plus GmmStatistics. The initialization
and the M-step (second file of the class) come in the next ticket: here a model is used with explicit parameters (InitParams = "").
Reference: reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/hmm.py lines 390-531 (GMMHMM: constructor, docstring), 593-732 (_init_covar_priors,
_fix_priors_shape, _check) and reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/_emissions.py lines 184-307 (BaseGMMHMM); its tests
reference/hmmlearn-0.3.3/src/hmmlearn/tests/test_gmm_hmm.py, test_gmm_hmm_multisequence.py and test_gmm_hmm_new.py; and the numbers
of oracle/golden/gmm_spherical.json, gmm_diag.json, gmm_tied.json and gmm_full.json (inference_cases; structure in oracle/README.md; do
not open the JSON files whole, they are large).

**Criteria**

- **C1** — Score, ScoreSamples, Decode (Viterbi and Map), Predict and PredictProba reproduce every inference case of the four oracle files
(spherical, diag, tied, full covariances; two states of two mixture components) with both implementations, several sequences
and a length-1 sequence included.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm16.InferenceParityTests`

- **C2** — Check rejects, with ArgumentException, missing or mis-shaped Weights, Means or Covars, weights rows that do not sum to 1, negative
variances (spherical, diag), non-symmetric or indefinite tied and full matrices; it accepts zero variances with a warning;
EffectiveCovarsPrior and EffectiveCovarsWeight give the hmmlearn defaults of each covariance type and the user's values when set.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm16.ValidationTests`

- **C3** — Sample returns observations of the right shape whose per-state mean matches the weighted mean of the components (statistically),
for the four covariance types, and the first state follows StartProb.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm16.SamplingTests`

- **C4** — After an E-step the statistics given to DoMStep satisfy the invariants and equal the weighted sums computed in the test: PostSum sums
to the number of samples and each PostMixSum row sums to its PostSum entry, MN and CN are filled only when 'm' and 'c' are in Params,
MN starts at MeansWeight * MeansPrior; GetNFitScalarsPerParam gives the documented counts for the four types.
 · `dotnet run --project tests/HmmLearn.Tests -- --filter-class HmmLearn.Tests.Acceptance.Hmm16.StatisticsTests`

- **C5** — The code stays readable: short methods, no dead code, the XML summaries match the behaviour. · judged by the reviewer
